# Privacy Policy for TriPulse

  **Last updated: March 30, 2026**

  ## Data Collection

  TriPulse does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  ## What TriPulse Accesses

  TriPulse reads the following system information **locally on your device only**:

  - CPU usage
  - RAM usage
  - GPU usage

  This data is displayed in real-time on your menu bar and is **never sent to any server**.

  ## Third-Party Services

  TriPulse does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

  ## In-App Purchases

  TriPulse uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your
  payment information.

  ## Contact

  If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at:
  https://github.com/aaron-hsu/tripulse-support/issues

  ## Changes

  We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
